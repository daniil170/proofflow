# ProofFlow collaboration instructions

## Product validation context

Before proposing product, marketing, or validation work, read:

1. `ops/00 Dashboard.md`
2. `ops/01 Strategy/Product Thesis.md`
3. `ops/01 Strategy/ICP.md`
4. `ops/02 Validation/30 Day Plan.md`

Treat the Markdown files under `ops/` as the operational source of truth for the current validation cycle.

## Evidence rules

- Distinguish observed behavior from hypotheses and opinions.
- Do not count likes, views, compliments, or hypothetical willingness to pay as validated demand.
- Preserve customer quotations verbatim.
- Do not promote a product feature into active development unless it blocks a real pilot, repeats across at least three relevant users, or directly affects payment/publication.
- During the 30-day validation cycle, prioritize acquiring materials, delivering cases, publication, and payment over feature expansion.

## Documentation updates

- Update CRM records after outreach, interviews, and pilot state changes.
- Put research patterns in `ops/04 Customer Research/Insights.md` only when their evidence is linked or named.
- Record material product decisions in `ops/05 Product/Decisions.md`.
- Keep planned work separate from observed completion.
- Never mark a checklist item complete without user-provided or directly observed evidence.
